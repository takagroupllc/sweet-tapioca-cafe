Good to know

Coffee questions, answered

Is Sweet Tapioca Cafe a coffee shop?

We're a boba tea cafe that takes its coffee seriously. Alongside 50+ handcrafted drinks, we pour six cold coffees, hot coffee, a chocolate hazelnut coffee latte, and ceremonial-grade matcha, hot or iced.

Where can I get Vietnamese iced coffee in Chesterfield, MO?

Right here. Our Viet Coffee is strong Vietnamese-style iced coffee with condensed milk, made to order at 123 Hilltown Village Center.

What is ube coffee?

Filipino purple yam meets bold cold coffee. Ube has a mellow, vanilla-like sweetness, and it turns the drink a deep purple.

Do you have dairy-free options?

Yes. Oat milk and almond milk substitutions are offered upon request for most drinks.

Can I order coffee ahead for pick up?

Yes. Order online for pick up, or walk in and order from our kiosk. There's free parking right out front.
